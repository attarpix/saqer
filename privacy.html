Your privacy

  • Saqer is totally free, with no ads.
  • No personal information is collected. We only keep the name you type (if you add one) and your IP address, to keep the scoring fair. Both stay private.
  • The name you type is shown on the public scoreboard, so don't use your full real name.

Play fair

  • Scores made with cheat codes or without really playing, and offensive names, will be removed.
  • Political opinions have no place in the game and won't be tolerated.

Keep it fun

Saqer is made for fun and positivity, and we'd love to keep it that way. Please support it by staying fun and respectful toward us and our beautiful country.

Using the art

Feel free to use any of Saqer's art, as long as it isn't used to disrespect anyone.

By saving a score, you agree to these terms.
